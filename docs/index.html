fixBib

GitHub

Clean, verify, and format your BibTeX references for aerospace and fluid dynamics publications.

Upload a .bib file or paste directly from Overleaf. fixBib checks every entry against CrossRef and Semantic Scholar, with OpenAlex as a fallback. It automatically standardizes references to match aerospace publications, enables toggling between abbreviated and full journal names (like J. Fluid Mech. vs Journal of Fluid Mechanics), cleans author formatting, and exports beautifully aligned entries. Everything occur 100% in-browser — your bibliography never leaves your machine.

How it works

1

Upload or paste

Drop your .bib file or paste directly from Overleaf. Everything is parsed in your browser — nothing is uploaded to any server.

2

Verify

Each entry is looked up by title against Semantic Scholar, CrossRef, and OpenAlex. Authors, year, venue, DOI, and other fields are compared to the published record to correct incomplete or missing data.

3

Review & edit

Results are color-coded: Verified means everything matches, Auto-Updated shows a diff you can accept or edit inline, and Needs Review flags possible errors. A live preview shows the corrected BibTeX in real time.

4

Download

Download or copy the corrected .bib file with your changes applied. Ready for LaTeX, Overleaf, or any reference manager.

Features

Aerospace Formatting

Formats AIAA papers to @misc with howpublished paper numbers, compatible with AIAA, JFM, etc.

Clean & Beautify

Aligns field operators, strips junk tags, fixes casing, and groups fields logically.

Citation Key Generator

Toggles unified citation keys formatted by author name, year, and key title words (e.g. roy2025direct).

Journal Abbreviation

Toggle between abbreviated forms and full names for major fluid mechanics and aerospace journals.
